Monthly mean Landsat Collection 2 Level 2 surface temperature (Landsat 5 TM, 7 ETM+, 8 TIRS and 9 TIRS-2) sampled at 54,396 points spaced 30 m along the mapped course of the Vistula River, Poland, for every month from May to November, 2000–2024 (175 monthly composites; cloud, cloud-shadow and non-water pixels removed with the QA_PIXEL band). Produced in Google Earth Engine with the script 02_Centerline_Points_Export.js (https://github.com/asadjutt3180/vistula-landsat-river-temperature).

Files:
- Vistula_LST_monthly_centreline_composites_2000_2024.zip – seven CSV files (one per month), columns Date, Year, Month, Point_ID, Longitude, Latitude, OSM_ID, River_Name, LST_Celsius (°C), River_Km (distance along the OpenStreetMap line), Source_File, Month_Name.
- Vistula_centreline_points_30m_shapefile.zip – the 30 m centreline points (shapefile, WGS 84) used as the Earth Engine asset.
- .sha256 files – checksums.

These data underlie Sections 3.2–3.3 (monthly composites, coverage-controlled trends, August 2020, longitudinal thermal profile) of: Basheer, A.; Amnuaylojaroen, T.; Ashraf, M.N.; Ptak, M. Landsat Thermal Calibration for River Temperature Monitoring: A 25-Year Case Study of the Vistula River. Water (MDPI).
