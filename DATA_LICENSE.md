## Third-party data

**IMGW-PIB (in-situ water temperature and water level, `data/raw/insitu_imgw/`).**
Obtained from the public data service of the Institute of Meteorology and Water Management – National Research Institute, https://danepubliczne.imgw.pl. Re-use is subject to the IMGW-PIB terms of use, which require the attribution:

> Źródłem pochodzenia danych jest Instytut Meteorologii i Gospodarki Wodnej – Państwowy Instytut Badawczy. Dane IMGW-PIB zostały przetworzone.
> (Source of the data: Institute of Meteorology and Water Management – National Research Institute. The IMGW-PIB data have been processed.)

The files contain the daily records of May–November 2000–2024 at the gauges Toruń, Chełmno and Gdańsk-Świbno, converted from the original download to CSV without changes to the values.

**Landsat Collection 2 Level 2 (`data/raw/landsat_station/`, Zenodo composites).**
Derived from U.S. Geological Survey Landsat data, which are in the public domain. Courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey.

**River centreline (Zenodo shapefile).**
Derived from OpenStreetMap data, © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence (ODbL, https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/).
